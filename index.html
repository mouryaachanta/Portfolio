About Me

Engineering Foundation to Applied AI

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate from Sasi Institute of Technology & Engineering (CGPA: 7.3). With a strong analytical grounding in signals, mathematics, and systems, I identified my deep passion for extracting actionable patterns from complex data streams and transitioned squarely into Data Science and Machine Learning.

My practical experience centers on building end-to-end predictive pipelines using Python, SQL, and modern deep learning frameworks. In my capstone research, I tackled industrial Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation on the benchmark NASA C-MAPSS dataset, formulating a hybrid 1D CNN-Transformer architecture with probabilistic uncertainty quantification, which was accepted and published in the peer-reviewed Advanced International Journal for Research (AIJFR).

To continually elevate my professional standards, I am currently enrolled in the rigorous Scaler Data Science & Machine Learning program (Bengaluru, India), strengthening my skills in advanced relational SQL, statistical data modeling, and production-grade AI deployment.
